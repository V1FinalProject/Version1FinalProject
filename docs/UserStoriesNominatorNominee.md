# User Stories — Star Awards Recognition Platform

*Version 1.0 · 20 August 2026 · Aligned to Requirements v3.0*
*Format: As a \<role\> I can \<capability\> so that \<benefit\>.*

---

## Nominator

**US-05 — Submit a nomination**
As an employee, I can submit a Star Award nomination for a colleague using the same form I use today, with my identity and their practice and location filled in automatically, so that I can recognise someone without the process asking more of me than the recognition itself.
*Traces to: SUB-01, SUB-02, SUB-03, SUB-04, ELG-01, ELG-02*

**US-06 — Resubmit after a rejection**
As a nominator whose nomination was not approved, I can submit a replacement nomination in the same quarter using my restored entitlement, so that a rejected nomination is not the end of the road for the recognition I wanted to give.
*Traces to: REV-05, ELG-04*

**US-07 — Respond to a clarification request**
As a nominator whose submission has been queried, I can open a side-by-side view showing my original nomination alongside the reviewer's question and submit a revised version, so that I can provide what the reviewer needs without my original text being lost or my entitlement being consumed.
*Traces to: REV-06*

---

## Nominee

**US-08 — Be told when a nomination is approved**
As an employee who has been nominated, I can receive an email telling me a nomination has been approved, showing me what was written about me and who wrote it, so that I feel genuinely recognised rather than just informed.
*Traces to: COM-01, SEC-01*
